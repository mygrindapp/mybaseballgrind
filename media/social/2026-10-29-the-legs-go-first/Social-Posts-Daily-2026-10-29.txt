The legs go first.

Last week of October. On the MyGrind training calendar this is peak strength month, the heaviest lifting of the off-season before November shifts gears.

For girls who play baseball, the off-season weight room works the same as it does for everyone else on the roster. Lower body power is where bat speed, throwing velocity, and first-step quickness start.

The part that gets skipped is writing it down. The Workout entry in MyGrind sets the focus, suggests the lifts, and saves one top set per session. That is how a personal best becomes something your player can see, not just remember.

In 35 years of coaching, more games never made a player stronger. The work between them did, and the players who kept a record of it showed up in March knowing exactly what they had built.

Tonight, one line: log today's top set. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
