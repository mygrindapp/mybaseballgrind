--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead, scheduled 7:30 PM) ---

Fall ball is over. The last game deserves a page, not a postgame speech.

Friday real talk for parents: the car ride home is where a lot of us want to replay every at-bat. Most players already know how it went. What they need is a quiet minute to write it down themselves.

Let your player answer two questions in their journal. What happened, and what did I do for my team? What's the goal next time? Then ask just one thing: "What do you want to work on this winter?"

In 35 years of coaching, the players who kept growing were the ones who owned their own review. More games won't do that for your player. What they do between games will.

Tonight, one line. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
