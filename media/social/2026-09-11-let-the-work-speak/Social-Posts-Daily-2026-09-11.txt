=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

A question parents of girls who play baseball ask me every fall: how do I make sure my player gets a fair look?

Here is the honest answer from 35 years in dugouts. The fairest look a player gets usually comes from a conversation they start themselves.

Not a parent email. Not a talk in the parking lot after a loss. Your player walking up to the coach and asking: "Here is what I have been working on. What do I need to do to earn more innings?"

That question lands differently with a record behind it. Six weeks of entries. The ground balls, the bullpens, what the coach said last time and what they did about it.

A journal does not guarantee innings. Nothing does. It does make sure the work is seen, and it teaches your player to own the conversation. That lasts longer than any lineup card.

More games will not start that talk. What your player writes down between them will.

Tonight, one line.

Coach
