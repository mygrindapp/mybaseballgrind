--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (girls-who-play-baseball lead, scheduled 7:30 PM) ---
(No URL in the Meta caption: FB Page 2-link/month cap.)

The car ride home can wait.

Fall ball is done, and a lot of us have the season review ready before the seatbelt clicks. Playing time, at-bats, that one inning. We mean well. It usually lands as one more grade.

Try it the other way this year. Before you say anything, let your player write the recap first. One page: what got better, what still bugs them, what they want to work on this winter.

For girls who play baseball, this matters even more. They hear plenty of opinions from the stands. Their own words about their own season are the ones worth keeping.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who kept improving were not the ones who played the most games. They were the ones who could tell you, in their own words, what happened between them.

Then read it together. You will learn more from their page than from any lineup card.
