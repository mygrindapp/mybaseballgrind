The semester grade is the one that counts.

Mid-November. First-quarter grades are posted, and a lot of families read Q1 like a final. It isn't. Q1 is the progress check. The semester grade is the one that goes on the transcript and into the NCAA core GPA, and it is being written right now, one class at a time.

That means a B in chemistry today is still a conversation, not a verdict. There are weeks left to move it.

In 35 years of coaching, I've watched a lot of players chase one more showcase while a core class slipped. More showcases don't fix a transcript. What your player does between them does: knowing where every class stands, and doing something about the one that's light.

The Academics tab in MyGrind lists each class, tags it core or elective, and shows the grade next to it. It is included with every plan.

Tonight, one line: the class that needs the most attention, and one thing your player will do about it this week. (Link in bio.)
