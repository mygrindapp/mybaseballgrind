--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

The box score is half the page.

For a lot of softball families this is the last fall ball weekend. Your player will come home with a line: 1 for 3, a double, a walk, a stolen base. Log it. The app does the math (AVG, OBP, SLG update on their own).

Then write the other half. One sentence on what they learned. "Sat back on the changeup in the 4th." "Got the bunt down when it mattered." "Struck out looking, chasing nothing next time."

In 35 years of coaching, the players who improved over the winter were not the ones with the best October stat lines. They were the ones who could tell you why.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does, and it starts with writing tonight down while it's fresh.

Tonight, one line. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
Coach
