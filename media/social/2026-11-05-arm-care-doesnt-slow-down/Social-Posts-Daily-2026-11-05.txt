--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (baseball lead, scheduled 7:30 PM) ---

Throwing slows down in November. Arm care doesn't.

Fall ball is winding down and most arms are getting a well-earned break from hard throwing. Good. That break is part of the plan. But a break from throwing is not a break from the shoulder.

Thursday tip: keep the small stuff going. Arm circles, a back-of-shoulder stretch, 8 minutes of J-Bands. No field, no bucket of balls. It fits in the garage before dinner. If the arm hurts, skip the bands and talk to a trainer.

In 35 years of coaching, the arms that felt good in March usually belonged to the players who kept looking after them in November.

More games won't do that for your player. What they do between games will.
