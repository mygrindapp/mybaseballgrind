--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

The off-season has no box score.

October is quiet. No standings, no stat sheet, nobody in the stands. It is also the heaviest strength month of your player's year: the lifting peaks, the bat stays busy with tee work and front toss, and the arm gets its plan.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. And in these months, almost all of it happens where nobody is watching.

So the journal becomes the box score. A few lines a day: what they lifted, how many swings, how the arm felt. By February that is four months of proof in their own words, not a story about working hard all winter.

I laid out the whole year month by month, in season to the February ramp, on the blog (link in bio).

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
