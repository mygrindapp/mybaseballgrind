(No URL in the Meta caption: FB Page 2-link/month cap.)

Too much? Stop guessing.

Thanksgiving week is here, and so are the holiday tournaments. Three games Friday, two Saturday, a showcase Sunday. Every parent in the stands is asking the same quiet question: is this too much for my player's arm?

Most families answer it with a feeling. "They said they're fine."

In 35 years of coaching, the players who stayed healthy and kept improving were not the ones who played the most games. They were the ones whose week was written down, so the adults around them could see the load and call a rest day before the arm called it for them.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, and how they recover, does.

MyGrind totals the throws and pitches from your player's journal and reads the last 7 days against age-based guidance (Pitch Smart, ASMI). Guidance, not medical advice. But a number beats a guess.

Tonight, one line: how many throws today. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
