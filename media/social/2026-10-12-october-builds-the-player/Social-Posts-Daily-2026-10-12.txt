--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Spring gets the crowd. October builds the player.

Fall ball is winding down. The bleachers empty out, the schedule goes quiet, and a lot of families hear "off-season" and think "later."

In 35 years, here's what I've watched. The players who showed up in March a step ahead didn't play the most games in the fall. They used the quiet months on purpose: arm care, tee work, one honest note about what still needs work.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does, written down.

Not a big plan. Tonight, one line: one thing fall ball taught them, and one thing they'll work on this week.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
