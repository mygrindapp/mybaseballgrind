Bat speed starts in the middle.

Mid-November. Fall ball is over and most players think the work is too. This is the month the swing gets built, and the piece almost everyone skips is the core.

A stable core is what carries the power from the legs to the barrel. When it is weak, the hips fire and the energy leaks out before it ever reaches the ball. It looks like an arms swing. It is usually a middle problem.

The fix is not glamorous. Pallof press, dead bugs, side planks. Ten minutes, three days a week, all winter.

In 35 years of coaching, the hitters who showed up in February with a faster bat were not the ones who played the most games in the fall. They were the ones who did the boring work between them, and could show you on paper that they did it.

Tonight, one line: the three exercises your player did and how many sets. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
