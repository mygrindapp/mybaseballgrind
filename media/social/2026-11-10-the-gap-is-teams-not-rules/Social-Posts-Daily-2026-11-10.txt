The gap is teams. Not rules.

Every season a parent asks me some version of the same question, usually quietly, after practice: my player loves baseball, how long do they get to play it?

The honest answer: girls play baseball at every level. Little League, high school, college club ball, a national team, and a pro league that started this summer. Nothing requires your player to switch to softball.

What shows up around age 12 is a gap in available teams, not a rule. November is the month to work on it. Fall ball is done and spring teams are next, so this is when you look.

I wrote the full map, every level and where to find girls baseball teams near you. It's on the MyGrind blog: "Can Girls Play Baseball?" (Link in bio.)

While your player looks for the next team, the work keeps going. One line tonight about what they did today. That page is theirs no matter which roster they land on.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
