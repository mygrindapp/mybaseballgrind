--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

Nobody claps for a November rep. Spring does.

Fall ball is wrapping up. No bleachers, no scoreboard, no team chat buzzing after the game. This is the stretch where a lot of players quietly set the bat bag down until February.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who showed up different in spring didn't play more games over the winter. They spun a few balls against the garage wall, hit off a tee under the porch light, and wrote down what they did. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

No one is watching those reps. That's exactly why they count. And when your player reads the page back in March, they'll see the work nobody else did.

Tonight, one line: one thing your player worked on today, even if it was ten minutes.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
