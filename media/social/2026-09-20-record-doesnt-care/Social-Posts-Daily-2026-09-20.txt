INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION
================================================================
In 35 years around this game I have watched a lot of players get sized up before they ever swung.

Girls who play baseball know that feeling early. They walk onto a field and get looked at before they get watched.

I stopped arguing with it a long time ago. Arguing does not move a coach. A record does.

What actually moves people is simpler than a highlight reel. Your player shows what they did between games. The reps counted for the week. The days in a row they showed up. The two things they were working on, and what happened when they tried them Saturday.

That is evidence, and it travels. Into a tryout. Into a playing time conversation. Into a recruiting email. It does not need anyone's permission to be true.

More games will not make your player better. What they do between games, written down, is the part that separates them.

Fall ball is running right now. The season is in front of them, not behind them.

Tonight, one line. That is the whole ask.

Coach
