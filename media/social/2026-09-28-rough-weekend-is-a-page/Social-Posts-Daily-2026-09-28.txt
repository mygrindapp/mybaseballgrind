--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

One rough weekend is a page, not the whole book.

Fall ball weekends can be long. Maybe your player went 0 for 8. Maybe the circle felt a mile wide and the walks piled up. Maybe they sat more than they wanted to.

In 35 years I have watched a lot of softball players have a weekend like that. The ones who kept improving did one simple thing on Monday: they wrote it down, found one thing that went right, and picked one thing to work on this week. That's it.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does.

Tonight, one line. One thing that went right, one thing to work on.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
