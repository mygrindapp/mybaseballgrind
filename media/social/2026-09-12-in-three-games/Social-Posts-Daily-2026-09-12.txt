Fall ball Saturday. Your player will play today, and by Tuesday most of it will be gone.

That is not a memory problem. That is just how games work. Three games from now, today blurs into the pile with the rest of them.

Here is the part that survives: the one thing they wrote down.

After the game, before the phone comes out, your player opens the journal and logs two things. One moment that actually happened. One adjustment they want to try next time.

Not stats. Not a box score. Somebody else is already tracking those. This is the part nobody else is keeping.

Thirty five years of watching players, and the ones who climbed were almost never the ones who played the most games. They were the ones who took something out of each one.

More games will not do that on their own. A note will.
