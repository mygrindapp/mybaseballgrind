--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Coaches don't recruit your best day.

They recruit the week. The ordinary one. The Tuesday nobody watched.

When a college coach finally sits across from your player, the question is
almost never about the highlight. It is: what does a normal week look like for
you? The players who can answer that clearly, with something written down,
sound completely different than the ones guessing.

That is true for every player, including the girls on baseball rosters. Same
question, same answer, same record.

September is the right time to start. Not because anything gets decided this
month, but because a season of normal weeks is the one thing you cannot go
back and create in June.

Tonight, one line. What they worked on, how it felt. That is it.

Log it. Own it.
