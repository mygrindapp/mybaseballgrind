The stretch nobody logs.

It is late November. Fall ball is done and the winter strength work is starting. The piece almost every player skips is mobility.

Tight hips and a stiff upper back cap a swing and a throw before strength ever gets a chance. Ten minutes covers it: hip flexor stretch, T-spine rotations, the 90/90 hip stretch. Three nights a week, all winter.

I see this with the girls who play baseball on travel rosters every winter, and with every other player too. The ones who show up in February moving freely did not play more games in the fall. They did the quiet work between them, and they wrote it down.

More games don't build range. What your player does between them does.

Tonight, one line: the three stretches your player did and how long they held them. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
