--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (baseball lead) ---

Three questions. Two minutes.

Late October is when a lot of baseball families say they will start a journal "once the season starts." A good entry is not a diary, though. It is three questions.

What did I work on today?
Where did I stand out or struggle?
What am I taking into the next practice or game?

That is the whole entry in MyGrind. Two minutes after practice, in your player's own words.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who kept improving were not the ones with the most games on the schedule. They were the ones who could tell you what they worked on Tuesday and what they were fixing by Thursday. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

The full how-to is on the blog this week (link in bio).

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
