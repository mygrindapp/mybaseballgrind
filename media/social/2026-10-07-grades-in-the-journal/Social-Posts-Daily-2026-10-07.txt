--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Grades count too.

October is when the first progress reports land. For a lot of softball families, it is the first time anyone has looked at a grade since August.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. College softball coaches look at the transcript before they look at the swing. Most college softball money comes as partial scholarships, and academic aid is often what closes the gap. And the NCAA core GPA starts counting freshman year, not senior year.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. That includes study hall.

So the journal holds both. One line about the chem lab, one line about the 40 swings. MyAcademics keeps the grades and the core GPA right next to the reps, included with every plan.

Not a big commitment. Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
