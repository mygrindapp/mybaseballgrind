--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (girls-who-play-baseball lead) ---

"I'm fine" isn't a check-in.

Late October, and a lot of players are running on fumes. Fall ball weekends, school, practice, maybe a second team. Ask how they feel and the answer is almost always "fine." That goes for the girls who play baseball on fall rosters, their teammates, and every player in the dugout.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does, and that includes knowing when to rest. In 35 years, the players who kept improving learned to read their own body early.

MyGrind's Daily Check-In takes 20 seconds: sleep, energy, body feel, arm feel, head. It tells your player when to push and when to back off, and it gives you something better than "fine" to talk about at dinner.

Tonight, 20 seconds.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
