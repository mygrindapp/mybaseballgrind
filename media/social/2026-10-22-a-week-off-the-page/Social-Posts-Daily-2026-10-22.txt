--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

A week off the page is easy to lose.

Late October fall ball goes fast. A doubleheader on Saturday, two practices, some cage work on a Tuesday night. By the next weekend most of it is a blur. Ask your player what they worked on last Wednesday and the honest answer is usually "I don't know."

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does. In 35 years, the softball players who kept getting better could always tell you what last week looked like.

MyGrind shows your player their last 7 days in one place: days logged, entries, reps, and every practice and game by date.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
