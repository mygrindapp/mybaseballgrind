=== META CAPTION (FB page + IG @mygrindapp, scheduled 7:30 PM) ===

Sunday, and fall rosters are coming together. On plenty of them there is a girl playing baseball. Not softball. Baseball. Her family is not looking for a headline about it. They want the same thing every other family wants: a season their player gets to finish.

35 years in dugouts taught me that the thing which ends a season early is almost never the swing. It is the grades.

Every August the school year resets. That is the real app screen above, today's date and all. The cumulative GPA carries over. This year is still blank. Blank is the whole opportunity, and it closes faster than parents expect once assignments start stacking up.

That is why academics lives inside MyGrind, right next to the reps. Same screen, same habit, one place.

More games will not protect your player's season. What they handle between the games will.
