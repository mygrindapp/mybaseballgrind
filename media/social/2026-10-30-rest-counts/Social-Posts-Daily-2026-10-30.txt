--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (baseball lead) ---

Rest counts. Log the off day.

Fall ball is wrapping up. For a lot of players this is the first week since February with nothing on the calendar, and that can feel like falling behind.

It isn't. In 35 years of coaching, the players who burned out were rarely the ones who rested. They were the ones who never stopped long enough to notice they were tired.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does, and rest is part of that work.

MyGrind has an Off Day entry for exactly this. No practice, no game, no gym. Your player writes one affirmation, how they took care of their body, and who was in their corner today. It still counts toward the streak, because showing up for recovery is still showing up.

Tonight, one line: log the off day. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
Coach
