--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

October swings become March averages.

Most softball families treat October like a pause. High school season is months away, fall ball is winding down, and "we'll get serious in January" sounds reasonable.

After 35 years, here's what I've seen. The players who show up in March looking different did the quiet work in October. Heavy lifts while there's time to recover. 100 swings a day. One real rest day. Nobody was watching, so they wrote it down.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does, and October is all between.

That's why MyCalendar lays out every month, and why the journal is where your player logs what they actually did. Not a big commitment. Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
