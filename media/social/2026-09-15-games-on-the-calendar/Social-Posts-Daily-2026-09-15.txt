=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

Fall ball is here, and the calendar filled up fast.

Here is the part that gets missed. The games are already scheduled. The growth is not. Another weekend of baseball does not automatically make your player better. What they do between those games, written down, is the separator. In 35 years I have watched that one gap decide who jumped and who stayed the same.

It does not take much either. After the game or the practice, one line. What did you work on, and how did it go. Six weeks of that and your player can tell a coach exactly what they have been fixing, instead of guessing at it.

I wrote the full breakdown on whether fall ball is worth it, and what your player should actually be getting out of it:
