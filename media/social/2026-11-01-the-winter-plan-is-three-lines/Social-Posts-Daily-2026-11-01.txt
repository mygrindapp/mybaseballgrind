The winter plan is three lines.

It's November 1. Fall ball is wrapping up and spring feels a long way off, which is exactly when "we'll start later" costs the most.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who came back in February ahead of where they left were not the ones with the biggest plans. They were the ones who wrote down a few things they could measure, then kept looking at them.

So keep it small. Three lines your player can finish:

One for the body. "Long toss 150 feet by February."
One for the game. "Cut called strikeouts in half this spring."
One for school. "Hold a 3.5 GPA through the winter."

Girls who play baseball: same long toss, same at-bats, same report card, same three lines.

None of this takes more games. It takes what your player does between them.
