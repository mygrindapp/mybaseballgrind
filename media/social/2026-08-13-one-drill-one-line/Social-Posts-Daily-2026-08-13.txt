Thursday drill tip, and it has nothing to do with a bat.

After practice tonight, ask your player one question. What is the one thing you worked on today, and how did it feel?

Then have them write it down. Thirty seconds in the car.

35 years of coaching taught me the players who make the jump in August are almost never the ones who took the most swings. They are the ones who could tell you what they were fixing.

More games will not separate your player. Knowing what they worked on will.
