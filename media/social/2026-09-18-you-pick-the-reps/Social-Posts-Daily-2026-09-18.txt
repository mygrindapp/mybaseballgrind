=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

Fall ball is three weeks in, and some of you are watching your player sit more than you expected.

I want to say the honest thing about that.

You are not going to fix playing time from the bleachers. Not with a conversation in the parking lot, not by switching teams in October, not by being louder than the other parents. Coaches see what they see, and the lineup card is theirs.

Here is what is actually yours.

Between last Saturday and this one, your player has a stack of hours nobody assigned them. The tee in the garage. The long toss. The ten minutes of glove work before practice starts. Nobody is writing that down. Nobody is counting it.

Start counting it.

In 35 years I have watched a lot of players get passed over in the fall and start in the spring. It was never the parent who complained the most. It was the player who kept a record of their own work and showed up in February looking like somebody who never stopped.

Your player does not control the innings. They control the reps. And the reps are the part that carries into next season.

Tonight, one line. What they did, and one thing they would do differently.

Coach
