MECHANICS LEARNED ON A LOCKED MAC (carry these forward):
1. LOCKED + 0 Chrome windows is NOT fatal. `open -na "Google Chrome"
   --args --profile-directory="Profile 1"` (the YBG profile) brought a
   window up and the extension reconnected, restoring the whole CDP path.
   This is the fix for the 9/17 dead end. Try it BEFORE giving up.
2. Keystrokes never land while locked: the extension's `type` action AND
   `key` action both silently do nothing in the page. Do not use them.
3. execCommand('insertText') DOES write into the Meta composer. But each
   newline makes Lexical reconcile and reset the caret, so a multi-paragraph
   insert collapses to THE LAST PARAGRAPH ONLY. Verified with a three-word
   test: inserting "ALPHA\n\nBETA\n\nGAMMA" leaves only GAMMA.
   => On a locked Mac the Facebook body must be ONE single block with no
   newlines. That is why today's FB copy is a condensed one-block version.
   The full paragraphed caption above is the one Coach posts to IG.
4. Hashtags CRASH the Meta composer. Inserting a "#tag" string opens the
   hashtag autocomplete and blanks the whole page (reproduced 3 times).
   Today's FB post therefore ships with NO hashtag line. Do not retry this
   on a locked run; it costs a full composer rebuild each time.
5. The time fields cannot be arrow-walked with real keys while locked, but
   they DO respond to a dispatched KeyboardEvent('keydown', ArrowUp/Down)
   on the input[role="spinbutton"]. Loop until aria-valuenow hits the
   target. All 6 spinbuttons drove cleanly to 7:30 PM this way.
6. Read the PREVIEW pane, never the editor's innerText. innerText includes
   a mirror node and autocomplete dropdown text, so it reports doubled or
   inflated lengths that are not in the actual post. On X, the character
   counter (role="progressbar" aria-valuenow, percent of 280) is the
   honest read: it showed 77% = ~216 chars, exactly the expected length,
   proving the "doubling" seen on 9/16 was a reading artifact, not real.
