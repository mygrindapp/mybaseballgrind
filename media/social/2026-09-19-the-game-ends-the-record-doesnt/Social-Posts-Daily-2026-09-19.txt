=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

The game ends and everybody scatters. Gear in the trunk, snack bar, drive home, phone.

Here is the part most families lose.

Your player just spent two hours learning something about themselves. How they handled the first at bat after the error. Whether they sat back on the change-up. What the circle felt like in the fourth when their legs started to go. That information is sharpest in the twenty minutes after the last out, and most of it is gone by Sunday morning.

Ask any coach what separates the player who is better in October from the one who looks the same in March. It is almost never talent, and it is almost never how many games they played.

It is whether anybody wrote anything down.

More games do not make your player better. What they notice between games does.

So before the gear comes off tonight. One line. What happened, and one thing they would do differently next Saturday.

That is the whole habit. Five minutes on the bench, in the car, on the back porch. By November they have a record of a season instead of a blur.

Coach

*** THE FIX THAT MADE A LOCKED MAC POSTABLE (new, 2026-09-19) ***
The Mac was LOCKED all run (CGSSessionScreenIsLocked true, visibilityState
"hidden"). The old conclusion was "locked = no posting, save a draft." WRONG.
The real blocker was the BACKGROUND-TAB TRAP, not the lock.
  - While the composer tab was a BACKGROUND tab, the extension's type/click
    actions silently did nothing: text never appeared, React never registered
    it, and Publish / Finish later stayed aria-disabled="true".
  - Injecting text with document.execCommand('insertText') DID put text in the
    DOM, but React/Lexical/Draft.js never saw it, so every submit button
    stayed disabled. It also multiplied and scrambled the text whenever
    hashtags were present (hashtag autocomplete). DO NOT use execCommand to
    fill these composers.
  - FIX: front the tab with a Chrome-level AppleScript command
    (set active tab index / set index of window to 1). This is NOT a System
    Events keystroke, so it is SAFE while the screen is locked.
    After fronting, the extension's real click/type/key actions worked
    normally on BOTH x.com and Business Suite, React registered everything,
    and the full schedule flow (7:30 PM segmented time fields, arrow-walk
    method) completed end to end.
So: a locked Mac blocks only the NATIVE FILE PICKER (media attach). Caption,
scheduling, and posting are all fine once the tab is fronted.
