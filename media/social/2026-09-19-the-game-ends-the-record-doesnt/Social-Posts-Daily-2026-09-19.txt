=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

The game ends and everybody scatters. Gear in the trunk, snack bar, drive home, phone.

Here is the part most families lose.

Your player just spent two hours learning something about themselves. How they handled the first at bat after the error. Whether they sat back on the change-up. What the circle felt like in the fourth when their legs started to go. That information is sharpest in the twenty minutes after the last out, and most of it is gone by Sunday morning.

Ask any coach what separates the player who is better in October from the one who looks the same in March. It is almost never talent, and it is almost never how many games they played.

It is whether anybody wrote anything down.

More games do not make your player better. What they notice between games does.

So before the gear comes off tonight. One line. What happened, and one thing they would do differently next Saturday.

That is the whole habit. Five minutes on the bench, in the car, on the back porch. By November they have a record of a season instead of a blur.

Coach
