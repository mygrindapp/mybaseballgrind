35 years of coaching, and here is the pattern I keep seeing.

The players who got better were not always the ones who played the most games. They were not always on the best team. They were the ones who wrote down what they did between games: the swings, the throwing, what went wrong Saturday and what they would fix by Tuesday.

Fall ball is over now. The winter months are quiet, and that is exactly when the gap opens. One player waits for spring. Another keeps a page.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does.
