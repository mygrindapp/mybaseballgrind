=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

Fall ball is running. Here is a drill that costs nothing and fixes more than another bucket of swings.

Ten pitches. No swing.

Before your player takes their first cut in a session, they track ten pitches and call each one out loud. Ball or strike. That is it. Then they check how many they got right.

Most players who struggle in the box are not slow. They are guessing. Ten pitches of honest tracking tells your player something no swing count will.

Then they log it. How many they called right. What the pitcher was doing when they missed.

More games will not teach that. Ten quiet pitches and a written note will.

Tonight, one line.

Coach
