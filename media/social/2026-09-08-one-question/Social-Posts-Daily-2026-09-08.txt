=== INSTAGRAM CAPTION (@youngsbaseball, Coach's phone, 4x5 card) ===

Rosters are settled. Playing time is uneven. Right about now the 9:47 PM text
starts getting typed.

I have read hundreds of them over 35 years. Here is what happens on my end: I
stop reading at the comparison. Not because I do not care. Because the message
is not answerable, and a coach who just ran a four hour game has nothing good
left at that hour. Neither do you.

There is one question that gets a real answer from every coach I have ever
known. "What should my player be working on that they are not?"

That question cannot offend anybody. It hands the coach the job they actually
signed up for.

Then comes the part that changes anything. Your player writes the answer down
in their exact words, and logs what they did about it. Youth, high school,
girls who play baseball, softball, all of it. Same move.

The parents who change a coach's mind are never the ones with the best
argument. They are the ones whose player showed up two weeks later with the
thing fixed. Not feelings. Receipts.

Tonight, one line.
