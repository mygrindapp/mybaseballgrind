=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

35 years on a field, and the same thing kept showing up.

The players who made a real jump could always tell me what they were working on. Not what team they made. Not how many tournaments were on the calendar. They could name the thing: the front side, the first step, the change up that kept flattening out.

The players who stalled had a schedule. A full one. Games every weekend, showcases, travel. Busy is not the same as better.

That gap is the whole reason MyGrind exists. Your player writes down what they worked on and how it went, and a few weeks later they have an answer ready when a coach asks. The work was already there. The journal just makes it something they can see.

September is early enough in fall ball that this compounds.

Tonight, one line. What did you work on today, and how did it go.

Coach
