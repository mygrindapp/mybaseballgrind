--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (girls-who-play-baseball lead) ---

Recruiting starts with a record.

For girls who play baseball, the road after high school is not one well-worn path. College baseball, women's national team programs, club teams, showcases. Fewer people are watching by default, so the record has to come from your player.

October is a good month to start it. Fall ball is wrapping up, the first grades of the year are in, and winter workouts are next. Coaches will ask about reps, grades, and what your player has been working on. A record answers before they ask.

More showcases don't build a recruiting profile. What your player writes down between them does. In 35 years, the players who got a real look could tell a coach exactly what they had been working on.

MyGrind keeps it in one place: the daily reps, the grades and core GPA, and the recruiting and eligibility links coaches ask about.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
