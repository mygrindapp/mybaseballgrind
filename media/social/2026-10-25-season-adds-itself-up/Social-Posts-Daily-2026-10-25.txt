--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

The season adds itself up.

Ask a softball parent in late October how fall ball is going and you usually get a feeling. "Pretty good." "They're hitting better, I think." Feelings are fine. They just don't tell your player what to work on next.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who kept getting better were not the ones who played the most games. They were the ones who wrote down what happened in them. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

MyGrind keeps it simple: your player logs the game in the journal, and MyStats does the math. Average, on-base, slugging, ERA for pitchers, plus the reps behind them. By the end of fall, the season is right there in their own numbers.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
