Nobody claps on a Monday.

No crowd. No scoreboard. Nobody recording it. Just your player, a bucket of balls, and twenty minutes they did not have to give.

In 35 years I have watched a lot of talented players disappear by spring. The ones still standing were not the ones with the most games on the calendar. They were the ones who kept showing up on the nights nobody was watching, and who could tell you exactly what they were working on.

More games will not make your player better. What they do between games, written down, is the part that separates them.

Fall ball is live right now. The season is in front of them, which means this Monday counts, and so does the next one.

Tonight, one line. What they worked on, and one thing to fix. That is the whole ask.
