--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (girls who play baseball lead) ---

Confidence isn't a feeling you wait for. It's a page of work you can read back.

To the parents of girls who play baseball: fall ball is wrapping up, and the winter months are where a lot of confidence quietly gets built or lost. Not in games. In the reps nobody watches.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who walked into spring sure of themselves didn't get there by playing more games. They could open a notebook and see it: the bullpens they threw, the tee work they put in, the at-bat they figured out. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

That's what MyGrind is for. A place your player writes it down, so on the days the doubt shows up, the proof is already there.

Not a big plan. Tonight, one line: one thing fall ball taught them.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
