--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

A map for the dream. Never a fence.

Girls who play baseball hear a lot of noise about where they fit. Most of what parents read was written for somebody else's player.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. The game describes a complete player with five tools: hit, power, run, arm, field. Same five for baseball and softball, boys and girls. The benchmarks are a map. Where your player stands today is just the starting point on it.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. October is the month that work starts, while fall ball winds down and nobody else is counting.

So start the count. Tonight, one line in the journal: what they worked on and how it felt.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
