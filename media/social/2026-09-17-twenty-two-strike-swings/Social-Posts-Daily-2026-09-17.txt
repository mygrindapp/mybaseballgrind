=== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

Fall ball is in full swing, and here is the part nobody warns you about: your player is getting fewer at-bats than they did all summer. Bigger rosters, shorter games, more kids who need innings.

So the at-bats they do get matter more. And most of those are going to end up in a tough count.

Here is the drill. Twenty swings at home tonight, and every single one is a two-strike swing.

That means: choke up a half inch. Widen the stance a little. Shorten the load. Think line drive back up the middle, not over the fence. Twenty in a row, and no sneaking a big one in the middle of the round.

Ten minutes off a tee or with soft toss. It trains the exact at-bat fall ball keeps handing them.

This is for every player on a fall roster. Baseball, softball, and the girls who play baseball, who are in the same rotation as everybody else and need the same twenty swings.

Then log it. Not a paragraph. One line: "20 two-strike swings, felt short and quick by the last five."

Three weeks from now your player can look back and see they took 300 of them. That is what a coach means when they say somebody got better over the fall. It was never the games. It was the reps nobody watched.

Tonight, one line.

Coach
