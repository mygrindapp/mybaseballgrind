--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Log game 1 before game 2.

Fall ball Saturdays are doubleheaders, and the hour between games is the most wasted hour in softball. Snacks, sunscreen, phones, then right back out.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. The players who got better over a fall didn't play more games than anyone else. They used that hour. Two minutes in the dugout: what happened, and one thing to do next game. By game 2 they had a plan instead of a feeling.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. Sometimes "between games" is 45 minutes.

MyGrind's Game entry asks exactly two questions: what happened, and what's the goal for next game. Your player answers both before the car ride home turns it into a story.

Not a big commitment. Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
