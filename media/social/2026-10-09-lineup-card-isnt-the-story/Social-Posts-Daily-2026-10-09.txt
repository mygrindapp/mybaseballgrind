--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

The lineup card isn't the story.

Fall ball lineups move around. Your player starts one game, sits the next, plays a new spot on Sunday. Parents feel every one of those calls on the drive home.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. The players starting in the spring were rarely the ones who started every October game. They were the ones who treated a bench night like information: two good at bats off the bench, one thing to work on Tuesday, written down.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does.

MyGrind has a Reflection entry for exactly this. Your player picks how they feel (frustrated counts) and writes it honestly. No speech from the front seat required.

Not a big commitment. Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
