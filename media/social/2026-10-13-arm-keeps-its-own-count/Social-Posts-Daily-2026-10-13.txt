--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

Softball has no pitch count rule. Your player can keep one.

There is a myth in softball that the windmill is "free" and a pitcher can throw all weekend. The motion is easier on the elbow ligament than overhand pitching. The forces are still real, and most of softball has no universal pitch count rules to step in for your player.

And don't forget their other arm motion. Every overhand throw from the field runs on the same growing elbow and shoulder as a baseball player's.

Fall ball is winding down, which makes October the right month to start the arm's own record. A few lines a day: pitches, innings, lessons, how the arm felt. In 35 years, the arms I've watched stay healthy belonged to players who knew their own numbers.

The full arm care guide is on the blog (link in bio).

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
