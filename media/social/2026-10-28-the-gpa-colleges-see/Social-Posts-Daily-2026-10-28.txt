--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

The GPA colleges see is the core GPA.

Late October. First-quarter grades are landing this week or next, and fall ball is still on the calendar. It is an easy month for the report card to slide.

Here is what a lot of softball families find out late: the NCAA does not use the overall GPA. It uses core courses only. English, math, science, social studies, foreign language. Electives and weight training do not count toward it.

The Academics tab in MyGrind tags each class as core or elective, figures the NCAA core GPA, and shows where your player stands for D1, D2, D3, NAIA, and JUCO. It is included with every plan.

In 35 years of coaching, the recruiting conversations that went smoothly started with a player who already knew their numbers. More showcases don't build that. A few minutes each quarter, written down, does.

Tonight, one line: enter one Q1 grade. (Link in bio.)

Log it. Own it.
