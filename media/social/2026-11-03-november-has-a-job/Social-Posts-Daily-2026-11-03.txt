--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (softball lead) ---

November has a job.

Fall ball is wrapping up, the clocks went back, and a lot of softball families figure the next real date on the calendar is February. In the MyGrind training plan, November is the last full month of the strength build, and the week already has a shape: lifting days, daily swings, a Wednesday for mobility and arm care, and one full rest day. If your player pitches, that arm care day is not optional.

In 35 years of coaching, the players who came back different in the spring weren't the ones with the most games on the schedule. They knew what today's job was, and they wrote it down. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

The whole year, month by month, is on the blog (link in bio).

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
