--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

The transcript shows up before the swing.

End of September. Your player is about six weeks into the school year, and the first progress report is right around the corner. Fall ball is rolling too, so the grades are easy to lose track of.

Here's what 35 years of watching kids chase the next level taught me: when a college coach gets interested, one of the first things they ask about is the transcript. Before the velo. Before the exit speed. A great swing can't get your player on a roster the grades already ruled out.

The players who handled it well didn't cram in May. They checked in on their grades the same way they checked in on their reps: a little bit, every week, written down where they could see it.

Tonight, one line. How did school go this week?

Log it. Own it.
Coach
