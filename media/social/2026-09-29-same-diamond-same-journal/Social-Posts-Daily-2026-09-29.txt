--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Same diamond. Same reps. Same journal.

Late September, fall ball is rolling, and there is a girl on a lot of baseball rosters right now. Not softball. Baseball. Same 90 feet, same white ball, same bullpen, same long bus rides home.

Parents of girls who play baseball tell me the same thing: most of what they read is written for someone else. So here it is plainly. The work is the same, and so is what makes it count.

In 35 years the players who kept getting better, boys and girls, did one simple thing: after practice or a game, they wrote down one thing that went right and one thing to work on. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does.

I wrote a full guide on every level a girl can play baseball, from Little League to high school to college and beyond. It's on the blog (link in bio).

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
