--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

A map for the dream. Never a fence.

In 35 years of coaching baseball, I have watched a lot of numbers get used the wrong way. A radar gun reading at 13. A 60 time at a showcase. A kid hears one number and decides the ceiling is already set.

So when I wrote the five tools page in MyGrind, I wrote it the way I wish every player heard it. Hit, power, run, arm, and field are simply how the game describes a complete player. Same five for baseball and softball, boys and girls. The numbers are a map. Never a fence around your player.

Here is what actually moves a player on that map: the work between games. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

It is late September. Fall ball is here and the offseason is close. This is the stretch where the players I watched improve picked one tool and went to work on it.

Tonight, one line. Which tool, and what they did for it today.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
