--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (baseball lead) ---

The learning starts after the last out.

Late October, fall ball Saturday. Two games, maybe three, and by the drive home most of it has already blurred together. The at-bat in the third. The ground ball that ate them up. The pitch they finally laid off.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. In 35 years, the players who kept improving were the ones who wrote down one thing that worked and one thing to fix, before it faded.

That is all a MyGrind game entry asks: what happened, what you did for your team, and the goal for next game.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
