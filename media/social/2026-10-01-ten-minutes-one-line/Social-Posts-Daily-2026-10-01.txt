--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Ten minutes on their own. One line.

October. Fall ball is a new year. Team practice might be twice a week, and the rest of the week belongs to your player.

Here's the Thursday tip. Pick one drill they can do alone. Tee work in the garage: three sets of 15, slow and on purpose. Then open the journal, tap On My Own, and write one line. What did they work on? How did the last set feel?

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does. In 35 years, the players who improved most weren't the ones with the busiest schedule. They were the ones who put in ten quiet minutes on their own and wrote it down.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
