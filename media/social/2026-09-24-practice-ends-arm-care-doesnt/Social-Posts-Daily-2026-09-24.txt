--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

Practice ends. Arm care doesn't.

Fall ball is a new year. Arms are fresh, schedules are loose, and this is exactly when habits get set for spring.

Here is the Thursday tip. After practice, before the car ride turns into phone time, give it ten minutes: bands, wrist and forearm work, a slow stretch. Then open the journal and write one line. Was it with the team or on their own? What did they work on? How did the arm feel?

In 35 years, the players I watched keep improving were not the ones with the most games. They were the ones who took care of the small stuff between games, and could tell you about it.

Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
