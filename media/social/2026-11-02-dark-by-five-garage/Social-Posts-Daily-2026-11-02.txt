Dark by 5 doesn't end the work. It moves it to the garage.

The clocks went back this weekend. Fields go dark early, practice thins out, and November is when a lot of players quietly stop.

In 35 years of coaching, the ones who showed up different in spring didn't play more games over the winter. They found a tee, a net, a wall, and they wrote down what they did. More games don't make your player better. What they do between games, written down, does.

Your player doesn't need a facility or a big plan. Ten swings in the garage count if they make it onto the page.

Tonight, one line: what your player worked on after the sun went down.

Log it. Own it.
