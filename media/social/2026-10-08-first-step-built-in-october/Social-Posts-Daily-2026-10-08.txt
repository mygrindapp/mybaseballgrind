--- INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION ---

A quicker first step gets built in October.

Girls who play baseball already know the 90 feet to first does not care who is running it. Speed is speed.

Here's what 35 years of coaching taught me. The players who got faster were not the ones who ran the most. They ran short, ran hard, and rested fully between reps. Six 15 yard sprints. A few broad jumps. Lateral bounds. Twenty minutes, twice a week, while fall ball winds down.

More games don't make your player better. What they do between games does.

MyGrind's workout log has a Speed / Plyo focus with the drills, a form video for each one, and the why behind it. Your player taps what they did and writes one line about how it felt.

Not a big commitment. Tonight, one line.

Log it. Own it.
Coach
